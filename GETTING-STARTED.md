## 🚦 YOUR NEXT 3 STEPS

### 1️⃣ Convert HEIC to JPG (Required!)
Your folder has 51 HEIC files. Browsers can't display them.

**How to convert:**
- Visit: https://heictojpg.com
- Upload all HEIC files
- Download as JPG
- Replace originals in "Outfits Owned by Sankalpa" folder

⏱️ **Time needed:** 5-10 minutes

---

### 2️⃣ Tag Your Outfits
Open `tag-helper.html` with Live Server to tag all 164 images.

**In VS Code:**
1. Right-click [tag-helper.html](tag-helper.html)
2. Click "Open with Live Server"
3. Browser opens automatically
4. Tag each outfit (category, colors, style)
5. Click "Download outfits.json" when done

⏱️ **Time needed:** 30-45 minutes (but you can pause anytime!)

---

### 3️⃣ Launch Your Website!
Open `index.html` with Live Server.

**In VS Code:**
1. Right-click [index.html](index.html)
2. Click "Open with Live Server"
3. 🎉 Your outfit manager is live!

⏱️ **Time needed:** 5 seconds

---

## 🆘 Need Help?

**Live Server not installed?**
1. Go to VS Code Extensions (Ctrl+Shift+X)
2. Search "Live Server"
3. Click Install on "Live Server" by Ritwick Dey

**Images not showing?**
- Make sure HEIC files are converted to JPG
- Check filenames in outfits.json match actual files

**Want to test now?**
- The sample outfits.json has 3 dummy entries
- Open index.html to see how it works
- Then tag your real outfits to replace it
